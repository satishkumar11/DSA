# Deloitte DSA Interview Prep — 50 Problems

Sourced from: GeeksforGeeks Deloitte question bank, DSAPrep.dev Deloitte guide, LeetCode Discuss
interview threads (HashedIn/Deloitte SDE-1, Analyst Trainee, USI), and GfG interview experiences
(2024–2026). 🔥 = confirmed across 2+ independent sources → highest priority.

Research notes:
- Deloitte skews easier than FAANG: ~50% Easy, ~45% Medium, ~5% Hard.
- Heavy focus on **arrays/strings/hashing**, plus real Java/OOP questions and "explain your
  approach out loud" — communication matters as much as the code.
- SQL is ~30% of their question set (not counted below since you asked for DSA, but see note
  at the bottom — worth 30–60 min of prep).
- Cross-checked against https://www.hellointerview.com/learn/code — mark off what you've
  already covered there.
